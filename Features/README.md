# Features

## Key Features

The Hybrid Renewable Energy System combines renewable energy generation, energy storage, and IoT-based monitoring into a single integrated platform.

---

## Renewable Energy Generation

- Generates electricity using a **3 W Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Panel**.
- Produces bioelectricity using a **Micro-Algae Bio-Electrochemical Cell (BECC)**.
- Combines two renewable energy sources to improve system reliability.

---

## Intelligent Energy Management

- Amplifies low-voltage output using a **DC-DC Boost Converter** and **5 V Step-Up Module**.
- Charges an **11.1 V Lithium-Ion Battery Pack** for energy storage.
- Provides continuous power for small-scale applications.

---

## Real-Time Monitoring

- Measures output voltage in real time.
- Measures output current continuously.
- Calculates generated power automatically.
- Displays live electrical parameters on an IoT dashboard.

---

## ESP32-Based IoT System

- Uses an **ESP32 Development Board** for system control.
- Provides wireless monitoring through Wi-Fi.
- Accessible from any mobile phone or computer connected to the network.

---

## Automatic Cooling System

- Monitors system voltage continuously.
- Automatically activates a **12 V DC Cooling Fan** when required.
- Improves system safety and reliability.

---

## Sustainable Design

- Eco-friendly and renewable energy based.
- Low-cost prototype suitable for educational and research purposes.
- Demonstrates the integration of biological and solar energy harvesting.

---

## Experimental Performance

- Stable hybrid energy generation.
- Successful battery charging.
- Real-time monitoring of voltage, current, and power.
- Reliable operation under laboratory testing conditions.
