# Working

## Working of the Hybrid Renewable Energy System Using Algae and Solar Energy

### Step 1: Renewable Energy Generation

The system generates electrical energy from two renewable energy sources:

- **3 W Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Panel**
- **Micro-Algae Bio-Electrochemical Cell (BECC)**

The solar panel converts sunlight into electrical energy, while the micro-algae bio-cell produces a small voltage (approximately **0.01–0.02 V**) through photosynthetic and electrochemical redox reactions using copper and zinc electrodes.

---

### Step 2: Voltage Amplification

The voltage generated by the micro-algae bio-cell is too low for practical applications.

To increase the voltage, the output is passed through:

- DC-DC Boost Converter
- 5 V Step-Up Module

These converters amplify the voltage to a level suitable for battery charging.

---

### Step 3: Battery Charging

The amplified output from the algae bio-cell, together with the power generated by the solar PV panel, is used to charge an **11.1 V (3-cell) Lithium-Ion Battery Pack**.

The battery stores the generated energy and provides a stable power supply whenever renewable energy generation is low.

---

### Step 4: Real-Time Monitoring

The system continuously monitors its electrical parameters using:

- Voltage Sensor (Voltage Divider)
- ACS712 Hall-Effect Current Sensor

These sensors send voltage and current data to the ESP32 microcontroller.

---

### Step 5: Power Calculation

The ESP32 processes the measured voltage and current values and calculates the generated power using:

**Power = Voltage × Current**

The calculated values are updated continuously for accurate monitoring.

---

### Step 6: Automatic Cooling

To maintain safe operating conditions, the ESP32 continuously monitors the system voltage.

When the voltage exceeds a predefined threshold:

- The ESP32 activates a **12 V DC Cooling Fan**.
- The fan is controlled using an **L298N Motor Driver**.

Once the voltage returns to the normal operating range, the cooling fan is automatically turned OFF.

---

### Step 7: IoT Dashboard

The ESP32 hosts a **Wi-Fi-based Energy Dashboard**, allowing users to monitor the system remotely using any mobile phone or computer connected to the same network.

The dashboard displays:

- Output Voltage
- Output Current
- Generated Power
- Battery Status
- Cooling Fan Status

This enables real-time monitoring without additional hardware.

---

### Step 8: Experimental Testing

The prototype was tested under laboratory conditions.

Observed values:

- Voltage: **2.54 V**
- Current: **1.52 A**
- Power: **3.85 W**

The experimental results confirmed stable operation of the hybrid renewable energy system.

---

### Step 9: Continuous System Operation

The complete operation cycle is repeated continuously:

1. Generate electricity from the Solar PV panel and Micro-Algae Bio-Electrochemical Cell.
2. Boost the generated voltage.
3. Charge the Lithium-Ion battery.
4. Measure voltage and current.
5. Calculate output power.
6. Display real-time data on the IoT dashboard.
7. Automatically control the cooling fan.

This continuous process ensures efficient renewable energy generation, energy storage, and real-time monitoring for research, educational, and small-scale off-grid applications.
