# Hardware

## Hardware Overview

The Hybrid Renewable Energy System combines renewable energy generation, energy storage, sensing, and IoT monitoring using the following hardware components.

---

## Hardware Components

| Component | Specification | Purpose |
|-----------|---------------|---------|
| Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Panel | 3 W | Generates electrical energy from sunlight. |
| Micro-Algae Bio-Electrochemical Cell (BECC) | Copper & Zinc Electrodes | Produces bioelectricity through photosynthetic and electrochemical reactions. |
| ESP32 Development Board | Wi-Fi Enabled | Controls the system, reads sensor data, and hosts the IoT dashboard. |
| DC-DC Boost Converter | Step-Up Converter | Increases the low voltage generated by the algae cell. |
| 5V Step-Up Module | Voltage Booster | Provides sufficient voltage for battery charging. |
| Lithium-Ion Battery Pack | 11.1 V (3 Cells) | Stores the generated electrical energy. |
| ACS712 Current Sensor | Hall-Effect Sensor | Measures output current. |
| Voltage Sensor | Resistive Voltage Divider | Measures output voltage. |
| L298N Motor Driver | Dual H-Bridge Driver | Controls the cooling fan. |
| 12V DC Cooling Fan | Brushless DC Fan | Prevents overheating during operation. |
| Breadboard | Standard Prototype Board | Used for hardware assembly and testing. |
| Connecting Wires | Jumper Wires | Provides electrical connections between components. |

---

## Hardware Connections

- The **Solar PV panel** supplies electrical energy to the charging circuit.
- The **Micro-Algae Bio-Electrochemical Cell** generates a low voltage using copper and zinc electrodes.
- The **DC-DC Boost Converter** and **5V Step-Up Module** increase the algae cell voltage.
- The boosted voltage and solar output are used to charge the **11.1 V Lithium-Ion Battery Pack**.
- The **Voltage Sensor** and **ACS712 Current Sensor** send real-time measurements to the **ESP32**.
- The **ESP32** processes the data, calculates power, and displays the readings on the IoT dashboard.
- The **L298N Motor Driver** controls the **12V Cooling Fan** whenever the voltage exceeds the preset threshold.

## Hardware Features

- Hybrid renewable energy generation using solar and algae.
- Real-time voltage and current measurement.
- Battery charging and energy storage.
- Wi-Fi-enabled IoT monitoring using ESP32.
- Automatic cooling fan control.
- Low-cost and eco-friendly design.
